Ember Tide — keep your lantern lit: gather ember orbs, shatter shadow wisps across ten waves, pick a blessing between waves, and face the Tide King on wave 10. A complete arcade game in plain HTML and canvas JavaScript, no build step and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How to play

- WASD or arrow keys, or hold the mouse, to move your lantern.
- Bolts fire on their own at the nearest shadow.
- SPACE or SHIFT to dash through danger (it recharges).
- Gather ember orbs to build a combo; shatter wisps for points.
- Clear a wave to pick one of three blessings. Clear all ten waves to win.
- P pauses, M mutes, R restarts.
